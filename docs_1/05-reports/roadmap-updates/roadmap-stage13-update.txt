**Backend Implementation (90% Complete):**

**Created Files:**
1. **AdminRoles API** (~675 LOC):
   - `admin-role-detail.model.ts` (105 LOC) - GraphQL types & enums
   - `admin-roles.service.ts` (450 LOC) - Business logic
   - `admin-roles.resolver.ts` (120 LOC) - GraphQL operations

**Key Features:**
- **4 Role Types** with permission presets:
  - `SUPER_ADMIN` - All 40+ permissions
  - `ADMIN` - 26 permissions (most operations)
  - `MODERATOR` - 12 permissions (limited)
  - `SUPPORT` - 6 permissions (minimal)

- **8 GraphQL Operations:**
  - Queries: `adminRoles(role?, search?, limit, offset)`, `adminRole(id)`, `adminRoleByUserId(userId)`
  - Mutations: `assignAdminRole`, `updateAdminPermissions`, `updateTwoFactorEnforcement`, `updateIpWhitelist`, `changeAdminRole`, `revokeAdminRole`

- **40+ Granular Permissions:**
  - Format: `resource:action` (e.g., `users:view`, `teams:delete`, `settings:update`)
  - Categories: Users, Teams, Projects, Subscriptions, Payments, Settings, Storage, Roles, Support, Content, Analytics, Logs, System

- **Security Features:**
  - PermissionsGuard protection on all mutations
  - Safety check: Cannot revoke last SUPER_ADMIN
  - IP whitelist support (IPv4, IPv6, CIDR)
  - 2FA enforcement per role
  - Full audit logging to AdminActionLog
